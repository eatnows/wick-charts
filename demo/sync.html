wick-charts — pan/zoom sync

Two independent WickChart instances, kept in sync via setVisibleTimeRange/getVisibleTimeRange

Drag/zoom/scroll the top chart ("driver") — the bottom one ("follower") tracks it by time, not index, even though it has loaded a smaller window of history. This is the concrete use case setVisibleTimeRange/getVisibleTimeRange exist for: syncing two independent chart instances that share a time axis but not necessarily the same loaded range — see "Reading chart state" in the README.
